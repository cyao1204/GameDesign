## Decided
- **Turn speed by target** (§5.5): self-targeted spells resolve first, opponent-targeted spells last. No utility speed category. Alternating turns dropped.
- **Spells per turn** (§5.1): up to 2–3 ("up to N").
- **Resting** (§5.1): each empty slot takes 1 turn off a chosen spell's cooldown, and slots stack. Resting doesn't lower affliction.
- **Spell power costs** (§5.1): stronger spells have longer cooldowns and add more affliction.
- **Starter elements** (§6): one Offensive and one Defensive element, placeholder names. Fire + Frost rejected as cliché. Elements differ by their mix of spell roles (attack / defensive / utility).
- **Offensive element** (§6): can never lower its own affliction. It's rewarded by finishers (main), empowered spells, and an Overloaded transformation.
- **Chaos** (§6): third element, rule-breaking direction.
- **Health** (§5.2): health = pool − every spell in the book (active + reserve), at a fixed rate.
- **Deckbuilding room** (§5.4): prototype at 8 spells per element and 4–5 active slots; 12+ per element in the full game.
- **Affliction meter** (§5.3): 0–20, with fixed Calm / Charged / Overloaded zones, the same for everyone.
- **Open information** (§5.2): affliction meters and every cast spell are public. Everything else is hidden.
- **Double knockout** (§5.5): lower total affliction wins, then a draw.
