## Decided
- **8 Offensive spells, 6 attack / 0 defensive / 2 utility** (§6), sorted by **power tier**: attack 2 cheap / 2 medium / 2 strong, utility 1 cheap / 1 medium.
- **Unlocks removed and rolled into empowerment** (§5.3, §6). Every spell can always be cast; past a threshold, certain spells become empowered. Supersedes session 4's "spells castable only at Charged".
- **The empowerment threshold is a zone, set per spell** (§6): empowered from Charged (with a possible extra step at Overloaded), or from Overloaded only. **What empowerment does differs per spell.**
- **The Overloaded transformation is the Overloaded step** of Charged-empowered spells (§6). No separate transformation of the player.
- **Empowerment goes to cheap spells; strong spells are never empowered** (§6), so late games don't shrink to casting only strong spells.
- **Only attacks are empowered** in the Offensive element: both cheap attacks and one medium attack. **Overcommit is the empowered medium attack**, from Overloaded only.
- **Attacks include burst and damage over time**; the two cheap attacks are one of each (§6).
- **Strong attacks: Lockdown and Pact** (§6, Chris's designs):
  - **Lockdown:** damage, and one of the opponent's revealed spells goes on cooldown (or its cooldown gets longer).
  - **Pact** (name proposed): gain a set amount of affliction within N turns for set damage; miss it and take extra affliction. The goal is an amount gained, never a level. Borrowed Overloaded doesn't count, and the opponent can't block the goal.
- **Utility spells** (§6, Chris's designs):
  - **Cheap:** doubles the next spell's potency (burst: double damage; damage over time: double damage over the same turns), and doubles that spell's affliction on top of its own.
  - **Medium:** the caster counts as Overloaded for the next turn, **costs included**, and reverts at the end of the turn.
- **Cast order is selection order** (§6, answers **Q-020**). A doubler cast last doubles the next turn's first spell. Doubling a utility spell is allowed and wasted.
- **Going over 20 affliction loses the match** (§5.3) at the end of that turn, unless the opponent has been defeated by then. Fills the gap left by dropping burnout.

## Tentative
- **Medium attacks: Capacitor and Overcommit** (§6).
  - **Capacitor:** damage equal to the affliction gained since it was last cast.
  - **Overcommit:** recasts itself every turn, taking a cast slot, until broken.
- **Empowerment zones** (§6): both cheap attacks from Charged with an Overloaded step; Overcommit from Overloaded.
- **Tiebreakers** (§5.3, §5.5, answers **Q-022**). All are subject to change after playtesting:
  - **Both players go over 20:** more health left wins, then less total affliction, then fewer spells in the spellbook, then fewer rests (counted in empty cast slots), then a draw.
  - **One player goes over 20 on a double-knockout turn:** that player loses.
  - **Otherwise,** when both reach 0 health: less total affliction wins.
